## What I care about

The work I enjoy most sits at the boundary between backend engineering and product building.

- distributed systems and service design
- AI workflows that are grounded and practical
- developer tooling that reduces friction
- APIs and infrastructure that are easy to reason about 
- shipping software that is useful beyond the demo

## Working style

I try to build with a simple principle: make the architecture as clear as the product goal.

That means:

- keeping interfaces clear
- reducing hidden complexity
- shipping systems with room to iterate
- learning from real usage instead of guessing from theory alone
